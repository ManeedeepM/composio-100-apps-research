🔑 1. Auth Landscape & Dominance

OAuth 2.0 (58%) dominates user-facing systems (CRM, Support, Marketing, and Productivity). These require Composio's centralized token refresh, redirect handling, and multi-tenant app client setups.

API Key / Bearer Tokens (34%) dominate Developer Infra, Data Scraping, and AI tooling (Stripe, Firecrawl, Supabase, Reducto). These offer zero friction for automated agent actions.

Legacy & Hybrid (8%) require custom handling: AWS SigV4 + OAuth (Amazon SP-API), HMAC signatures (Binance), and local CLI execution (Mermaid CLI, Sherlock).

🚪 2. Gating vs. Self-Serve Distribution

71% of apps offer self-serve access via free tiers, automated developer sandboxes, or instant signups.

  • Developer & Infra (90% Self-Serve): Instant signups (GitHub, Supabase, Cloudflare, Sentry).
  • Finance & Ad Networks (60% Gated): Heavily gated behind corporate credit checks, enterprise sales, or ad-partner reviews (DealCloud, PitchBook, LinkedIn Ads).
  • Ecommerce (Mixed): Self-serve for open/creator platforms (Shopify Partner, Gumroad, WooCommerce) but high gating for Amazon SP-API and SFCC.

🎯 3. Composio Strategic Triage

Immediate Quick Wins (45 Apps): Modern REST/GraphQL APIs with self-serve API keys or standard OAuth2 (Linear, Coda, Firecrawl, Supabase, Reducto, Plain, ClickUp, Discord).

Core Expansion Wave (30 Apps): Standard SaaS apps requiring standard Composio OAuth App registration (HubSpot, Zendesk, Mailchimp, QuickBooks, Asana).

Enterprise / Outreach Partnerships (25 Apps): Requires developer partner applications or enterprise contracts (Amazon SP-API, LinkedIn Marketing, DealCloud, Gladly).
